# ZOE'S Pet Shop

## About the Project

ZOE'S Pet Shop is an online store focused on premium pet products, specialized nutrition and care products for animals with specific needs.

Our goal is to make high-quality products more accessible to pet owners, especially those looking for alternatives to regular commercial pet food.

The store will offer hypoallergenic food, specialized diets, nutritional supplements, dermatological care products and premium accessories. Some of these products are harder to find in regular pet shops and are often available in veterinary pharmacies.

The project is developed for the E-Commerce course, academic year 2026-2027.

## Product Categories

The store will include at least 30 products divided into 4 main categories.

### 1. Premium and Hypoallergenic Food
- Grain-free and limited-ingredient food
- Hypoallergenic food for sensitive pets
- Premium dry and wet food
- Food for specific dietary requirements

### 2. Specialized Nutrition and Supplements
- Veterinary-formulated diets
- Nutritional supplements
- Digestive support products
- Joint and mobility supplements

### 3. Hygiene and Specialized Care
- Dermatological shampoos
- Sensitive skin care products
- Grooming and hygiene products
- Specialized non-prescription care products

### 4. Toys and Premium Accessories
- Interactive toys
- Premium beds and blankets
- Collars and leashes
- Feeding accessories

## Target Customers

Our target customers are pet owners who want to provide better nutrition and care for their animals.

We especially focus on people who have pets with food sensitivities, allergies or specific nutritional needs and who may have difficulty finding suitable products in regular pet stores.

## Main Features

The website will allow customers to:

- Browse products organized by category
- Search for products by name
- View detailed product information
- Check prices and stock availability
- Create an account and log in
- Add, update and remove products from the cart
- Complete the checkout process
- Make simulated payments using Stripe
- View their order history

Administrators will be able to manage products, categories, stock and customer orders.

### Maxi Pet

- Product availability information
- Search and filtering options

### What Makes Our Store Different

While these stores offer a wide range of standard pet products, ZOE'S Pet Shop will focus more on premium and specialized products.

We want customers to find hypoallergenic food, specialized nutrition and care products in one place, without having to search multiple pet stores or veterinary pharmacies.
